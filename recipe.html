Masala Chai

A warm, comforting Indian tea brewed with milk, black tea and aromatic spices. Perfect for a rainy evening with a plate of biscuits.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 teaspoons black tea leaves
  • 2 teaspoons sugar
  • 1 inch fresh ginger, crushed
  • 2 black peppercorns, crushed
  • 2 green cardamom pods
