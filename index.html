My Favourite Movie: 3 Idiots

About the Movie

3 Idiots (2009) is a Hindi comedy-drama directed by Rajkumar Hirani. It follows three engineering students and questions the pressure of marks and rankings in the education system. I love it because it is funny, emotional and reminds me to chase excellence, not just success.
